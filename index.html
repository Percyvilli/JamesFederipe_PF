01AI · INFORMATION SYSTEM

MediSync

AI-assisted rare disease information and decision support system

My contribution · Authored the research manuscript and led system development, with a teammate contributing to implementation.

Problem & approach

An educational support system for people exploring rare disease information. It organizes possible considerations, supporting and non-fitting findings, test information, alternatives, limitations, and sources, and encourages consultation with a specialist. It does not establish a diagnosis or replace professional judgment.

Code evidence: API response parsing, cited sources, session-expiry and rate-limit handling, and fallback error states.

Evaluation

Evaluation snapshot · Study averages were 4.26 from medical respondents and 4.00 from IT experts. These reflect quality and usability feedback, not diagnostic accuracy.
